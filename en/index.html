05 FAQ

What funeral homes ask us

Do you work at weekends?

Yes, 7 days a week, including public holidays. For a Muslim burial, we know every hour counts.

How long before departure?

It depends on the documents and on hold space. We look for the first flight that fits the planned burial date.

Which documents should we prepare?

Usually: the death certificate, the certificate of non-contagion, the authorisation to close the coffin, the coffin sealing report, the body transport authorisation issued by the prefecture and an identity document of the deceased. Each consulate adds its own requirements: we send you the exact list for your country.

Which coffin is required?

A wooden coffin with a hermetically sealed inner zinc lining, as international transport requires. Some airlines also ask for a gas purification filter: we tell you when we book.

What does IATA accreditation change for you?

We book directly with the airlines, with no middleman: one contact, from quotation to landing.

How is the price set?

On quotation, before any commitment. It depends on the destination, the airline and the coffin, with no hidden fees.

Who does what?

You keep the family, the coffining, the consulate and the transfer to the airport. We book the flight, issue the air waybill, hand the deceased over to the airline and follow the flight until landing.

Do you work outside Alsace?

Yes. Our base is Basel-Mulhouse, and we also book from Lyon, Strasbourg, Zurich or any other airport that suits the case.

How do I open a first case?

Write to support@aurevafreight.com or fill in the form. No framework contract is needed: your first case is enough.

What if the flight is cancelled?

We tell you at once and look for the next flight, so you can reassure the family.
